Task: analyze the business and its financial trajectory.

- `business_overview`: what the company does and how it earns money, as far as the context shows. If the context only has a sector/industry label, say that and keep this short rather than filling it with generic sector description.
- `competitive_position`: what the metrics suggest about competitive strength (returns on capital versus peers, margin level and stability, growth versus peers). Note where the evidence is too thin to tell.
- `financial_trends`: the five-year story in revenue, profit, margins, returns, leverage, and cash conversion, including any break in trend.
- `claims`: the material factual statements behind the three sections above, each with its evidence ids and metric keys.
- `open_questions`: the questions an investor should answer from the annual report or management commentary before forming a view.
